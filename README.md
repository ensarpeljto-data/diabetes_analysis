# diabetes_analysis

Patients with positive diabetes outcome show significantly higher average glucose and BMI values compared to non-diabetic cases

BMI category grouping indicates overweight and high-BMI groups dominate among positive outcomes

Glucose level segmentation shows most diagnosed cases fall into diabetic or prediabetic ranges

Age and insulin averages are also elevated in the outcome-positive group

Feature aggregation confirms glucose and BMI as strongest indicators in this dataset
